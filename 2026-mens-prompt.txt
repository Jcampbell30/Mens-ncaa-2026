You are conducting a deep research and synthesis task to produce a high-confidence NCAA Men’s Basketball Tournament bracket.

## Objective

Build a complete March Madness bracket by:

1. Aggregating insights from top bracketology experts, analytics models, and reputable sports journalism
2. Reconciling conflicts using a rational weighting methodology
3. Producing a finalized bracket with a winner selected for EVERY game (Round of 64 through National Championship)

---

Use search queries based on the following sources:

* "Joe Lunardi bracketology site:espn.com"

* "Bart Torvik tournament projections"
* "Haslametrics upset picks NCAA tournament"

* Consensus bracket projections (Bracket Matrix)

## Methodology Requirements

### Step 1: Establish Baseline

* Use consensus bracket (Bracket Matrix) to define seeds and matchups

### Step 2: Apply Predictive Metrics

Evaluate each matchup using:

* Offensive/Defensive efficiency (KenPom)
* Adjusted tempo and matchup fit
* Recent form (Haslametrics momentum)
* Win probability (TeamRankings or similar models)

### Step 3: Identify Upsets

* Select a SMALL number of strategic upsets per round
* Favor:

  * 10–7, 11–6, 12–5 matchups
  * Teams with strong efficiency but under-seeded
* Avoid excessive randomness

### Step 4: Weighting Logic (IMPORTANT)

When sources conflict, prioritize in this order:

1. Aggregated analytics models (KenPom, Torvik)
2. Consensus projections (Bracket Matrix)
3. Expert analysts (Lunardi, Palm, Athletic writers)
4. Narrative factors (injuries, coaching, experience)
